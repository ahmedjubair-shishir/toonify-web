# Toonify Web - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Toonify Web is a browser extension that restyles the websites you choose with a cartoon-style visual layer. It is built to work on your computer and to keep your information there.

## The short version

- Toonify Web does **not** collect, sell or share your personal data.
- It has **no** analytics, tracking, ads or accounts.
- Your choices are saved **only in your browser**.
- It contacts the internet only when **you** click to add an icon set (see below).

## What is saved, and where

Toonify Web saves these settings in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on your computer only:

- whether Toonify is on or paused;
- the websites you turned Toonify on for (their address, for example `example.com`, not full page addresses);
- the style, effects, icon set and icon look you picked for each of those websites;
- icon sets you added from the Icons tab (the icon drawings themselves).

The popup also remembers which tab you had open last.

Nothing of this is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the extension deletes it. "Reset site" in the popup removes a website's settings and Toonify's access to it.

## What Toonify Web does on websites

On websites you allowed, Toonify Web looks at the page's structure (for example: which elements are photos, buttons or icons, and the names sites give their icons) so it can style them. This happens inside your browser. Toonify Web does not read, store or send what you type, your messages, or page content.

## When Toonify Web uses the internet

Only in one case: when you open **Icons > Add new** and click a popular icon set, Toonify Web downloads that set's icons from **Iconify** (`api.iconify.design`), a free icon service. The request contains only icon names (such as "search" or "menu"). Like any website request, Iconify can see your IP address. The icons are then saved in your browser, and websites you visit never contact Iconify because of Toonify Web. See Iconify's own privacy information at https://iconify.design.

Fonts and the built-in icon sets come with the extension; they are not downloaded.

The **Feedback** link opens the extension's Chrome Web Store page (or its GitHub page) in a new tab. Anything you write there is handled by Google or GitHub under their own privacy policies.

## Permissions

- **storage**: to save your settings, as listed above.
- **activeTab**: to see the address of the tab you're on when you open the popup, so it can show that site's settings.
- **scripting**: to add the styling only to websites you allowed.
- **Access to websites (optional)**: asked for one website at a time, only when you click "Allow" for that website. You can remove it any time with "Reset site" or in Chrome's extension settings.

## Children

Toonify Web is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
